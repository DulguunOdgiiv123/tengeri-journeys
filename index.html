When to go

Twelve months, four different countries

Ulaanbaatar is the coldest capital in the world, yet summer on the steppe is warm and long. Pick a month to see the weather, the festivals and the journeys that fit.

Average daily low to high in Ulaanbaatar (1,350 m), °C. The Gobi runs warmer in summer; the north and the Altai run colder.

Experience

Life on the steppe, up close

Nights in family gers, herds on the move and traditions that have outlived empires.

Family stays on every journey
Stay with herders

The door of a ger always faces south

Guests sit on the west side, the stove burns at the centre and there is always milk tea. You help with the milking, learn to saddle a horse and pay your hosts directly.

  • 1

    Step over the threshold, never on it.

  • 2

    Accept a bowl with your right hand, the left lightly supporting the elbow.

  • 3

    Move clockwise inside the ger, following the sun.

The steppe in motion

Herds, horses and hunters

Herders still count their wealth in the five kinds of livestock: horses, camels, cattle, sheep and goats.
